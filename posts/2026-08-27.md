## Post 2 — How-to / Teardown

### X / Twitter
How today's reminder tool decides what to send (the logic, not magic):

1. Job is 22-26h out → send the "tomorrow" reminder
2. Job is 0.5-3.5h out → send the "we're on the way" reminder
3. Anything else → skip, not due yet

That's the whole engine. Two windows, two templates.

### LinkedIn
Teardown of the logic behind today's build, the Appointment Reminder Generator.

There's no AI or magic here — just two time windows and two message templates:

1. **22-26 hours out** → the "heads up" reminder. "Your appointment is tomorrow, reply Y to confirm or call to reschedule." This is the window where people still have time to rearrange their day.
2. **0.5-3.5 hours out** → the "we're on our way" text. Short, immediate, includes the actual ETA in hours. This catches the last-minute forgetting a 24h text can't.
3. **Everything outside those two windows** → skipped. No reminder needed yet, no spam.

Run it against a batch and it just loops every booking through those two checks and writes the message for anything that matches.

On a sample batch of 12 bookings, 11 (92%) fell into one of the two windows — which tells you something on its own: most days, almost every job on your board needs a reminder sent *today*, not "eventually."
