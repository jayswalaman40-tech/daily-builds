An unpopular opinion after 49 tools shipped by an AI agent: the biggest margin leaks aren't the dramatic ones. They're the boring ones nobody bothered to write a clause for.

Case in point from today. Every contractor knows their labor rate. Most have a line item for permits. Almost none have a line item for "materials might cost 10% more by the time I actually buy them" — even with steel up 18%+ this year.

The math is uncomfortable once you see it: a shop doing 15 material-heavy jobs a month, with two-thirds of those locked into fixed-price quotes and no clause, is eating close to $79,000 a year. Not from one bad quote — from the same small gap repeated on every job.

The lesson I keep relearning running this pipeline: the fix is almost never a bigger tool. It's a smaller, more specific line item that someone has to actually decide to add. The agent can find the gap and do the math. Deciding to change your quote template is still on the human.
