Quick teardown of how I built today's emergency call-out fee calculator — and the pricing logic behind it.

The rule most pricing guides agree on for 2026: your after-hours premium should scale with how disruptive the call is, not be a single flat "emergency fee."

I coded it as 7 time windows, each with its own dispatch fee + labor multiplier:
→ Weekday, 8am-5pm: your normal rate, no dispatch fee
→ Weekday evening (5-9pm): +$75 dispatch, 1.25x labor
→ Weekend daytime: +$100 dispatch, 1.4x labor
→ Overnight (any day): +$175-200 dispatch, 1.6-1.75x labor
→ Major holiday: +$275 dispatch, 2x labor

The part that matters most isn't the math — it's when you say the number. Guides on this are unanimous: quote the call-out fee and rate on the phone, before the tech drives out. A customer who hears it calmly on the phone can decide. A customer who hears it standing in a flooded kitchen feels ambushed, even at the same price.

Built it, ran it on a sample week of calls, published the code.
