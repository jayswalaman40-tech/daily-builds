## Post 3 — How-to / Teardown

### X / Twitter
How to grade (and fix) your speed-to-lead in 4 steps:

1. Log it — timestamp every lead in + first response out for 1 week
2. Bucket it — under 5min / 5-30 / 30-60 / never
3. One unanswered lead = automatic F. Fix that first
4. Then push the "under 5 min" bucket toward 80%+

Speed is the cheapest lead source you already paid for.

### LinkedIn
If you want to know whether your business is actually fast to respond to leads (not just "usually"), here's the 4-step version of the tool I built today.

1. Log it. For one week, timestamp when every lead comes in and when someone first responds — text, call, whatever counts as contact.

2. Bucket the results. Under 5 minutes, 5-30 minutes, 30-60 minutes, and "never responded." Most owners are surprised by how much sits in that last bucket.

3. Fix the zero-response leads first. In my sample run, one un-contacted lead was enough to drag the entire SLA grade to an F — worse than a business that's merely slow on every lead. A ghosted lead is a total loss, not a slow win.

4. Once nothing is going unanswered, push your "under 5 minutes" share up. That's the bucket the research on inbound lead response consistently points to as the highest-leverage one for conversion.
