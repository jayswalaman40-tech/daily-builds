Why it matters

Callbacks quietly eat your margin

🚨

2-3% is the benchmark

Best-in-class shops run callback rates under 1.5-2%. Many home-service businesses run 5-10% without realizing it.

💸

Every callback is unpaid work

A callback trip typically costs $300-$650 once you count windshield time, tech labor, and redone parts — and you don't get to rebill for it.

📉

It hides in the schedule

A callback also bumps a billable job off today's schedule — the real cost is the callback cost plus the job you didn't get to run.

✅

It's a process fix, not luck

Tracking callback rate by tech and by job type is usually enough to find the pattern and cut the rate in half.
