Why it matters

Turnover is a hidden margin leak

📉

Trade turnover runs 12%-21%

Depending on the trade, contractors lose roughly 1 in 6 to 1 in 5 techs every year — often without ever pricing in the cost.

💸

Replacing a tech isn't cheap

Recruiting, a 6-10 week open req, and 4-8 weeks to full productivity can add up to 0.5x-2x their annual salary.

🧾

It never hits the P&L directly

Turnover cost shows up as missing margin, overworked crews and missed jobs — not as a line item you can point to.
