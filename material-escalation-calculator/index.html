Why it matters

A price increase you don't pass on is a pay cut you gave yourself

📈

2026 tariffs are real and ongoing

Steel mill products carry a 50% tariff and are up over 20% year-over-year; aluminum and lumber have moved sharply too. This isn't a one-time blip.

📄

Fixed-price quotes lock in the risk

Without an escalation clause or tariff line item, you priced the job on yesterday's material cost — and you're on the hook for today's.

🧾

A small line item covers it

Industry guidance puts a defensible tariff/escalation line item at roughly 1.0–1.5% of total job value — easy to justify, easy to document.

✅

It's fixable on your next quote

Add the clause or line item going forward and this leak closes immediately — no negotiation with existing customers required.
