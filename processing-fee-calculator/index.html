Why it matters

Processing fees quietly eat thin margins

💳

Most owners never check the rate

Flat-rate processors often run 2.6%-3.5%. Interchange-plus pricing — the true wholesale cost plus a small, negotiable markup — typically lands near 2.1%-2.5%.

📉

It hits thin-margin trades hardest

At a 10% net margin, a 3% processing fee on a card sale eats roughly 30% of the profit on that job — before you've paid for anything else.

🧾

Surcharging and dual pricing are options

Passing some of the fee to card-paying customers (via a surcharge or dual cash/card pricing) is legal in most states, capped around 3%, and can offset a real share of the cost.

✅

It's usually just one phone call

Interchange is identical across every processor — the markup on top is the only negotiable part, and it's often the easiest margin you'll ever recover.
