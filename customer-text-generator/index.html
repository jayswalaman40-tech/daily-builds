Text Generator

Ready-to-send customer texts, in one tap

The right text at the right moment wins jobs and reviews — but nobody has time to write them mid-job. Pick a moment, fill in a couple of blanks, and copy a message that's ready to send.

How to use it

From "I'll text them later" to sent in 10 seconds

1

Fill in your details once

Your business name and the tech's name. Add the customer's name and time if you have them — the messages fill in automatically.

2

Pick the moment

Missed call, on my way, running late, job done, or a quote follow-up. Each gives you a friendly and a professional version.

3

Copy and send

One tap copies the text. Paste it into your phone and hit send — no typing while you're standing in someone's driveway.

Why it matters

A quick text is the cheapest way to win the job

📵

Save the missed call

A fast "sorry we missed you" text catches the caller before they dial the next contractor on the list.

🚚

Fewer no-shows

An "on my way" text cuts confusion and no-shows, and makes you look organized and professional.

⭐

More 5-star reviews

Follow-ups that close
