Why it matters

Flat-rate habits quietly erase margin

📋

Old price sheets don't update themselves

Labor and material costs creep up. A flat rate that made 30% margin two years ago might make 5% today.

🔧

Quick jobs are the easiest to underprice

Drive time, dispatch cost and small parts eat into short jobs fastest — they need the tightest math, not the loosest.

📉

A few underpriced jobs a week add up fast

Even a $30-50 gap per job, repeated dozens of times a month, is real revenue you never see.

☎️

Fast, consistent quotes win more jobs

Customers call multiple companies. Whoever responds fast with a confident, correctly-priced number tends to win the job.

Never let a slow callback lose the job either
