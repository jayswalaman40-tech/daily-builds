Why it matters

The IRS doesn't send a warning shot

🧾

Nobody withholds it for you

As a 1099 or sole-prop owner, you pay both halves of Social Security and Medicare yourself — 15.3% before income tax even starts.

📅

The IRS wants it quarterly

Owe $1,000+ for the year and you're expected to pay estimated tax four times a year, not once in April.

💰

25-35% is the real range

Most owners under-set-aside because they guess a flat 15-20%. A growing shop's true rate is often closer to a third.

✅

Fix it at the point of sale

Move your set-aside percentage to a separate account the moment a job is paid — not at tax time when it's already spent.
